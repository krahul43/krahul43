## Selected Projects

### 4eightLifeCoaching
A family-focused life coaching mobile application emphasizing structure, privacy, and personalized household experiences. Built with React Native, Expo, Firebase, and Redux Toolkit. Key features include household roles, privacy controls, family dashboards, and premium onboarding flows.

### Drink Subscription App
A subscription-based venue discovery platform where users redeem daily free drinks via QR scanning. Designed three distinct user experiences (subscriber, venue manager, admin) with Firestore, Stripe-style subscriptions, role-based navigation, and maps integration.

### Indian Railway Tracking App
A modern mobile concept for train tracking and station discovery. Focused on creating a polished, contemporary take on railway information with real-time tracking, timetables, location intelligence, and potential native capabilities.

### BaseCamper
A map-first outdoor discovery application for finding wild camping and outdoor locations. Emphasizes location-based UX, user-contributed spots, highlights, and modern visual design built on Firebase/Supabase-style architecture.

### "Should I Buy This?"
A decision-making calculator helping users evaluate major purchase decisions (cars, homes, education, phones). Transforms complex financial questions into interactive, visual calculations and comparisons.

### WaitlistKit
A SaaS concept for indie hackers and founders. Features waitlist landing pages, referral invitations, referral rewards, and viral leaderboards to power launch growth mechanics.

I believe simple ideas, when polished well, become products people enjoy using. The goal is seamless: ideas that feel effortless to use because they were thoughtfully built.
